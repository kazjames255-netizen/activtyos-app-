Build any listing

A guided builder for camps, classes, blocks, trips or subscriptions — dates, ages, capacity, ratios and questions, all in one flow.

Flexible booking

Let parents book a single session, a whole week, a discounted block or a rolling subscription — you decide what’s on offer.

Easy cancellations

Parents cancel within the rules you set; refunds go back to card or straight to their wallet credit — no awkward messages.

Memberships for parents

Offer membership tiers that give wallet credit or a standing discount — applied automatically at checkout, every time they book.

Sessions fill to the cap you set, then a waitlist forms — free up a space and the next family is offered it automatically.
