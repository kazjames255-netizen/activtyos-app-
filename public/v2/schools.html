Bringing it in-house

You’ve taken wraparound back. Now run it properly.

With families expecting cover from 7:45am to 6pm, many schools now run breakfast, after-school and holiday clubs themselves instead of handing the site to an outside provider. That’s better for parents — and a lot more admin for the office.

The office

Stop being the booking system

No more paper slips in book bags, cash tins, cheques or a staff member fielding “can I add Tuesday?” at the front desk. Parents book and pay themselves, and every payment is recorded against the child’s booking.

The clubs

Registers that stand up

Who’s in, who’s absent, who collected them and at what time — on a phone at the door, with allergies, medical notes and SEND flags on the child’s row rather than in a folder in the office.

The trust

One view, every school

Central sees bookings, income and unpaid balances across the trust; each school still runs its own clubs, its own staff and its own parents without seeing anyone else’s.

Parents & payment

Paid up front, without the chasing.

Parents book on their phone and pay the way they already pay for childcare — and the office sees exactly who owes what.

Card, vouchers & Tax-Free Childcare

Parents pay by card, employer childcare vouchers or from their government Tax-Free Childcare account. Each child gets their own payment reference, so every payment can be matched to the right booking.

Arrears you can actually see

A live list of who’s unpaid, by how much and for how long, with payment reminders sent before the due date. No spreadsheet to keep.

Funded and free places

Offer funded or free places at £0 — they’re booked like any other place and still appear on the register.

Book the term, or just Tuesday

Sell a whole half-term block, a week or a single session — with capacity, cut-off times and waitlists handled for you.

Siblings, in one basket

Families book every child onto every club in a single checkout and pay once. Sibling discounts apply automatically once you’ve set the rule, so nobody has to ask the office.

Receipts the office doesn’t write

Every payment produces a receipt in the parent’s account, dated and itemised — which is what they need for their employer or their childcare account, and what you need at year end.

Safeguarding, staff & compliance

The paperwork an inspection asks for.

The clubs either side of the school day are held to the same standard as the rest of it. This is where that evidence lives.

Incidents & accidents, logged

Written up on the spot with what happened, who was there and what was done — then shared with the parent where you choose, and kept, timestamped.

Medication & consent

Inhalers, EpiPens and prescribed medicine recorded against the child with the parent’s written permission — and a dose can’t be logged without it.

Safer recruitment records

DBS, references, right to work, qualifications and induction tracked per staff member, with expiry dates flagged ahead of time, a “cleared to start” check, and a rota that refuses anyone whose DBS has expired.

Policies read and confirmed

Publish safeguarding, behaviour and first-aid policies with version history, assign them to roles, and see who has actually read and confirmed the current version.

Rotas, cover and hours

Publish the club rota to TAs, coaches and playworkers, fill gaps when someone’s off, and let clocked hours feed straight into a timesheet the finance office can sign off.

Training that’s on file

Assign safeguarding, food hygiene, paediatric first aid and the rest from a built-in course library, and keep completions and certificates against the staff record.

Questions schools ask

The honest answers.

Does this replace our MIS?

No, and it doesn’t try to. Your MIS stays the record for the school day — admissions, attendance, assessment. Activly runs everything either side of it: the clubs, the bookings, the payments, the club registers and the staff who cover them. Lists and registers export as CSV for anything you need to move across.

Can parents pay with Tax-Free Childcare or vouchers?

Yes. Alongside card payments, parents can pay from a government Tax-Free Childcare account or with employer childcare vouchers. Each child has a unique payment reference, so when the money arrives you can match it to the right booking and tick it off.

We’re a trust with several schools — how does that work?

Central gets a trust-wide view of income, bookings, unpaid balances and safeguarding, and can switch features on or off for every school at once. Each school is set up as its own site under your head-office account and can only see its own families. Permissions are set per person, so each member of staff sees only what their role needs.

Who is the data controller?

You are. Family and child data belongs to the school or trust that collected it — it isn’t pooled, sold or shared with other providers. Data is held in line with UK GDPR, encrypted in transit and at rest, and our processing terms are published in full.

Our clubs are staffed by TAs, not a separate team.

That’s the common case. Staff are added once, given the roles they need, and rota’d onto clubs around the school day. Their DBS and training are tracked against their name, and clocked hours become a timesheet for the finance office rather than an email.

What does it cost?

A flat monthly fee — never a percentage of what parents pay you, so a full half-term never costs you more. See pricing, or book a demo and we’ll work through your provision.
