Bringing it in-house

You’ve taken wraparound back. Now run it properly.

With families expecting cover from 8am to 6pm, more schools are running breakfast, after-school and holiday clubs themselves instead of handing the site to an outside provider. That’s better for parents — and a lot more admin for the office.

The office

Stop being the booking system

No more paper slips in book bags, cash tins, cheques or a staff member fielding “can I add Tuesday?” at the front desk. Parents book and pay themselves, and the money reconciles against the child.

The clubs

Registers that stand up

Who’s in, who’s absent, who collected them and at what time — on a phone at the door, with allergies, medication and SEND flags on the child’s row rather than in a folder in the office.

The trust

One view, every school

Central sees take-up, income and unpaid balances across the trust; each school still runs its own clubs, its own staff and its own parents without seeing anyone else’s.

Parents & payment

Paid up front, without the chasing.

Parents book on their phone and pay the way they already pay for childcare — and the office sees exactly who owes what.

Card, vouchers & Tax-Free Childcare

Parents pay by card, employer childcare vouchers or from their government Tax-Free Childcare account. Each child gets their own payment reference, so the money matches itself to the right booking.

Arrears you can actually see

A live list of who’s unpaid, by how much and for how long, with reminders that go out on their own. No spreadsheet, no reconciling a bank statement against a paper register.

Funded, free and staff places

Mark places as funded, free or staff-discounted and they come off the invoice automatically — while still appearing on the register and in your take-up figures.

Book the term, or just Tuesday

Sell a whole half-term block, a repeating weekly place or a single ad-hoc session — with capacity, cut-off times and waitlists handled for you.

Siblings, in one basket

Families book every child onto every club in a single checkout and pay once. Sibling discounts apply themselves, so nobody has to ask the office for them.

Receipts the office doesn’t write

Every payment produces a receipt in the parent’s account, dated and itemised — which is what they need for their employer or their childcare account, and what you need at year end.

Safeguarding, staff & compliance

The paperwork an inspection asks for.

The clubs either side of the school day are held to the same standard as the rest of it. This is where that evidence lives.

Incidents & accidents, logged

Written up on the spot with what happened, who was there and what was done — then sent to the parent and kept, timestamped, for as long as you need it.

Medication & consent

Inhalers, EpiPens and prescribed medicine recorded against the child with the parent’s written permission, visible to the staff on that register — and to nobody else.

Safer recruitment records

DBS, references, right to work, qualifications and induction tracked per staff member, with expiry dates that chase themselves and a clear “cleared to start” gate before anyone is put on a rota.

Policies read and confirmed

Publish safeguarding, behaviour and first-aid policies with version history, assign them to roles, and see who has actually read and confirmed the current version.

Rotas, cover and hours

Publish the club rota to TAs, coaches and playworkers, fill gaps when someone’s off, and let clocked hours feed straight into a timesheet the finance office can sign off.

Training that’s on file

Assign safeguarding, food hygiene, paediatric first aid and the rest from a built-in course library, and keep completions and certificates against the staff record.

Questions schools ask

The honest answers.

Does this replace our MIS?

No, and it doesn’t try to. Your MIS stays the record for the school day — admissions, attendance, assessment. Wigglekit runs everything either side of it: the clubs, the bookings, the payments, the club registers and the staff who cover them. Lists and registers export as CSV for anything you need to move across.

Can parents pay with Tax-Free Childcare or vouchers?

Yes. Alongside card payments, parents can pay from a government Tax-Free Childcare account or with employer childcare vouchers. Each child has a unique payment reference, so when the money arrives it matches itself to the right booking rather than landing in a suspense account for someone to unpick.

We’re a trust with several schools — how does that work?

Central gets a trust-wide view of income, take-up, staffing and safeguarding, and can push a policy, price or club template out to every school. Each school still runs its own provision and can only see its own families. Permissions are set per person, so a business manager, a club lead and a DSL each see what their role needs.

Who is the data controller?

You are. Family and child data belongs to the school or trust that collected it — it isn’t pooled, sold or shared with other providers. Data is held in line with UK GDPR, encrypted in transit and at rest, and our processing terms are published in full.

Our clubs are staffed by TAs, not a separate team.

That’s the common case. Staff are added once, given the roles they need, and rota’d onto clubs around the school day. Their DBS and training sit on the same record, and clocked hours become a timesheet for the finance office rather than an email.

What does it cost?

A flat monthly fee — never a percentage of what parents pay you, so a full half-term never costs you more. See pricing, or book a demo and we’ll work through your provision.
