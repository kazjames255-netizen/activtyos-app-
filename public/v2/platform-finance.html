Tax-Free Childcare & funding

Government money, tracked against every booking.

HMRC pays by BACS with a short reference, often batched across several families, and the amount rarely equals your invoice. Activly gives every booking and child its own payment reference, shows what is still outstanding by family, and lets you tick each payment off against your bank statement.

How a payment lands
  1. Parent pays into their Tax-Free Childcare account
  2. HMRC adds 20% — up to £500 per quarter, per child
  3. BACS arrives with the child’s TFC reference
  4. Matched by its reference to child and booking
  5. Booking marked paid and the balance updates
  6. Shows in payouts against your own bank account
