Reach the right families

The right message to exactly the right list.

One place for every conversation — a single parent, a session’s families or your whole list — with the AI to help you write it.

What’s included

Message centre — 1:1 & group chat

Every conversation in one inbox — message a single parent or a whole group, and keep the history against the family.

Email campaigns to chosen audiences

Send to a chosen audience — booked families, everyone on a listing or your whole list — and see who opened it.

AI writes your messages

Describe what you need and the AI drafts the message or email for you — tweak the tone and send in seconds.

Newsletters & announcements

Post to your newsfeed for updates everyone should see — term dates, weather calls, celebrations — in one place.

Merge fields

Personalise at scale with fields like child name, session and venue — every family gets a message that reads like it’s just for them.

Consent-aware

Opt-outs are respected automatically, so marketing only reaches families who’ve said yes — you stay on the right side of the rules.

What’s included

Carries your logo and brand colour and opens from any phone browser, so parents see your business.
