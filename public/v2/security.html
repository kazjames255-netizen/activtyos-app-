Security & trust

Built to protect the data you hold.

You’re trusted with children’s information — allergies, medical needs, safeguarding notes. We take that seriously, with security built into the platform and a clear split of responsibility so you always know where your data stands.

Get started →Read our Privacy Policy
How we protect it

Security measures, in plain English.

How we protect it

Encrypted everywhere

Every connection is secured with HTTPS/TLS, and data is encrypted at rest on the infrastructure that stores it.

Your data is walled off

Each provider’s data is logically isolated — one provider can never see another’s families, staff or finances.

Least-privilege access

Role-based permissions mean each person sees only their part — the office sees money, coaches see only their sessions.

Reputable hosting

Runs on Google Cloud / Firebase infrastructure in UK/EU regions, which holds recognised security certifications.

Passwords never stored

We never see your password — it’s stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider.

Backups & resilience

Regular backups guard against loss and support restoration, with monitoring to catch issues early.

Payments handled by Stripe

Card payments run through Stripe (PCI-DSS certified). Card numbers are never stored on our systems, and money lands in your own account.

Breach response

We have procedures to detect and respond to incidents, and will notify the ICO within 72 hours where required, and you without undue delay.

People & process

Staff are bound by confidentiality, trained on data protection, and access data only where they need to.

Compliance

UK data protection, done properly.

UK GDPR & DPA 2018

We’re built to align with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. You are the data controller for your families’ and staff data; we act as your processor under a Data Processing Agreement you sign at set-up.

Safeguarding, inspection-ready

DBS tracking, live ratios, allergy/medical/SEND flags, medication locks and read-and-confirm policies — the evidence you need at inspection, built in.

Transparent & documented

Clear Privacy Policy, Terms and DPA, a named sub-processor list, and data export whenever you want it.

Certifications on our roadmap

We follow security best practices aligned to recognised standards, and formal certifications (such as ISO 27001 and Cyber Essentials) are on our roadmap as we grow. Status: in progress.

We describe the measures we take, but no system is ever completely secure. You help too — keep your login safe and give staff only the access they need.
