Your family’s data

Handled the way you’d want it handled.

You’re trusting this with medical notes and photographs of your children. Here’s the deal.

  • ✓
    Only your provider sees it. Your details belong to the provider you booked with. They’re not pooled, sold, or handed to anyone else.
  • ✓
    Only the staff who need it. Medical and safeguarding information is shown to the people running your child’s session — not to everyone with a login.
  • ✓
    Photos are consent-first. If you say no to photography, your child is excluded from Moments — and staff see that flag before they take the shot.
  • ✓
    UK data protection. Data is held in line with UK GDPR, encrypted in transit and at rest, with our security and processing terms published in full.
  • ✓
    Yours to see and to take back. View what’s held about your family from your account, correct it, or ask for it to be deleted.
  • ✓
    Records that stand up. Registers, incidents and medication logs are timestamped and kept properly — which is as much for your protection as theirs.

Questions parents ask

Before you sign up.

Does it cost me anything?

No. Wigglekit is free for families — your provider pays for it. You only ever pay for the sessions you book, at the price they set.

My child’s camp or club isn’t on here.

You can only create an account against a provider who uses Wigglekit, so search for their name when you sign up. If they’re not there, tell them about us — they can start free.

Can both parents have access?

Yes. A second parent or carer can have their own login to the same children, so you both see the bookings, updates and photos — and either of you can book.

Can I use Tax-Free Childcare or vouchers?

Where your provider accepts them, yes — alongside card payments and any payment plan they offer. Your payment is matched against the booking, so nobody has to chase a reference.

How do refunds and cancellations work?

Your provider sets the policy and it’s shown before you pay. Where a refund or credit is due, it’s issued to your wallet or back to your card — and you can see exactly what was returned and when.

Do I have to download an app?

No. It runs in your phone’s browser and you can add it to your home screen in a tap, so it behaves like an app without the app store.

What if I don’t want photos of my child shared?

Turn photo consent off in your child’s profile. They’re then excluded from Moments and staff see the flag before anything is taken or posted.

How do I delete my data?

Ask from your account, or contact your provider directly — they’re the data controller. Some records, such as accident and safeguarding logs, must legally be retained for a period; everything else can go.
