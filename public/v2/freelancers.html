What’s included

Build any listing

A guided builder for camps, classes and term blocks — dates, ages, capacity, ratios and questions, all in one flow.

Flexible booking

Let parents book a single session, a whole week or a discounted block — you decide what’s on offer.

Easy cancellations

Parents cancel within the rules you set; refunds go back to card or straight to their wallet credit — no awkward messages.

Memberships for parents

Offer membership tiers that give wallet credit or a standing discount — the discount is applied automatically at checkout.

Discount codes & referrals

Run discount codes and reward referrals — stack the rules you allow and watch redemptions in one place.

Waitlists & capacity

Sessions fill to the cap you set, then a waitlist forms — switch on automatic offers and the next family is offered any place that frees up.
