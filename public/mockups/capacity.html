What a parent sees. The calendar only marks exceptions — full days and nearly-full days — so a normal week looks exactly as it does now.

The line under the calendar

Says the shape while there's room; produces a number only when it's scarce.

Before — the same sentence regardless of scope, so 60 reads as "60 free today":

● 60 spaces left

After — 60 across the whole camp vs 20 per day:

Whole-listing cap — 60

Up to 60 children on this listing

Per-day cap — 20

Up to 20 children each day
Only 4 places left that day

Scarce = the last fifth, or the last 3 on a small group. So an 8-child club warns at 3, a 60-child camp at 12.
