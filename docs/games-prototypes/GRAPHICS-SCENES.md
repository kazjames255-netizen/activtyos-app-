# Per-game scene briefs (use with the shared art kit; owner request)

PENGUIN SLIDE: An endless icy slope at dawn. Glacier walls on both sides with ice-crystal detail, distant snowy peaks, aurora ribbons. The 3 lanes are carved grooves in the ice, not dashed lines. Answers are frosted ice blocks that Pip smashes through (shards fly). Snow spray from Pip's belly-slide. Speed lines at high combo.

TURBO SLIDE: Keep the 3D road. Fill the horizon: a sunset city skyline of igloo towers, mountains and a giant moon. Roadside props rush past (lamp posts, snowmen, billboards with multiplication facts, ice arches). Proper cartoon cars with wheels, windows and brake lights; Pip in a rocket sled with visible flame exhaust. Stagger distant answer labels so they never overlap, and fade them in at mid-distance. Nitro = FOV stretch + blue flame + streaks.

POLAR DASH: A side-scrolling platformer with Alto's-style layered hills, day->dusk->night cycle across the level, pine trees, ice caves, frozen waterfalls. Answer bubbles are glowing soap-bubble orbs with a shine highlight. Make the game canvas full screen; controls are tap zones.

GRAMMAR RUNNER: An enchanted library-city at night: bookshelves as buildings, floating lanterns, paper birds. Lanes are a cobbled street. Word gates are stone archways with the word on a carved banner. The target class shows as a big glowing sign above the road, and changes with a whoosh and colour change.

SLED RUN: A real downhill: steep slope in perspective, pine forest, jumps and moguls, Pip on a wooden sled with a scarf trail. Letter gates are slalom flags/arches with big letters. Snow spray on turns. The LISTEN button is a speaker icon on a snowman (the sound is played automatically every time).

CONVOY RUSH: A side view of a canyon with a river far below, trucks driving from the left in the distance and getting bigger. The bridge gap is clearly measured with a ruler along the top. Planks are real wooden planks with the fraction burnt into the wood, drawn exactly to scale. Fraction text >= 24px. Truck falls = splash + cartoon rescue helicopter.

SINKING BERG: A dramatic sea at dusk with rolling waves, a giant iceberg with a scale on top, and the equation carved into the ice in big glowing letters. The water line visibly rises as danger grows; the screen tints red near the danger line. Operation tiles are floating ice floes. Wally the Walrus boss has a big animated body.

LINE-UP: A noir police line-up room. Each line of working is a "suspect" card held by a cartoon character walking across a height-chart wall under a spotlight. The escape line is a door on the far side. Tapping = spotlight slam + "GOTCHA!" stamp. No empty void: use the whole screen.

PORT DEFENCE: A living harbour: animated waves (layered sine shapes with foam), seagulls, a lighthouse sweeping its beam, lots of boats with pronoun flags at different distances. A proper cannon that recoils and smokes; cannonballs arc with a trail; hits make a splash and the boat sinks with bubbles. Colours shift to night as the level goes on.

STORM NIGHT: Make it an island town, not a chequerboard. A 3/4 view (isometric-ish) island in a dark sea, cottages with glowing windows, cobbled paths, and lantern light pools. Storm events: rain, lightning flashes, giant waves rolling at the sea wall; wall sections rise with stone-block animation when you answer correctly. Buildings get lit up as the town grows.

INTERROGATION: A moody detective office: rain on the window, a desk lamp cone, the suspect (a cartoon animal) across the table with animated expressions (sweating when you're close). The worked solution types out on a big chalkboard or typewriter paper. The OBJECTION! button is a huge comic-style speech burst with a screen flash and zoom. Big clock on the wall that ticks and turns red. Dark text on the yellow PLAY button.
