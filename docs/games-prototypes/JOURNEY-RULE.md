# The Journey Rule (owner decision, applies to EVERY game)

Owner (Kaz): "as great as these are kids will get so bored after say 5 goes. The theme needs to change as they progress."

## The rule
1. **No game is one look.** Every game is a JOURNEY of at least 5 themes (worlds), each with 4 or more stages. A child should see something new (new art, palette, weather, props, music mood and a new rule or hazard) about every 5 runs.
2. **Themes are swappable packs.** Game logic stays the same. A theme pack is one module: palette, background layers (parallax), weather, props, hazards or obstacles, music mood, the host character's outfit, and a short story line. Adding a theme must not need engine changes.
3. **Learning progress moves the journey, not time or grinding.** A new theme unlocks when enough content is secured (mastery), never by playing longer or paying.
4. **Each new theme adds something to DO,** not only a new skin: a new obstacle, mechanic, question form or boss. Skins alone get boring by the third theme.
5. **A map shows the whole journey** (visited, current, locked with a teaser) with stars for accuracy (never speed), so the next place is always visible and wanted.
6. **A boss or finale ends every theme,** with a distinct pattern.
7. **Return visits stay fresh:** a rotating challenge, a personal-best ghost, and spaced-review "visits" to earlier themes.
8. **Same ethics as always:** earned only, no random or paid rewards, no pressure timers, weekly rhythm with banked rest days, calm mode, Junior and Explorer looks.
9. **Cast as a series of hosts:** the same character can change outfit per theme, and other cast members can guest.

## Standard theme ladders (starting point)
- Fluency games: Glacier, Aurora Ridge, Crystal Caves, Storm Pass, Summit.
- Science games: Lab, Rainforest Station, Volcano Rim, Deep Sea, Space Station.
- Maths reasoning games: Harbour, Old Town, Clock Tower, Canal District, Skyline.
- Language games: Market, Coast, Old City, Mountain Village, Capital.
- History games: one theme per era band.
