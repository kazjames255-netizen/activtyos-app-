# Name TBC — Website Content Plan

---

## 1. Strategy in a page

**What we are.** Name TBC is the operating system for UK children's activity providers — the one place to sell activities, run the day, pay the team and grow a network. It scales on a single login from a solo coach to a national franchise. It is not a booking widget with extra tabs; it is the back office of a national operator, run from a phone.

**Who it's for.** Three audiences on one platform: the **freelancer** (solo coach, tutor, instructor), the **company** (multi-staff camps, clubs and wraparound care), and the **franchise** (a head office running a branded network).

**The core promise — three things rivals give up on.** (1) **Your money.** 0% commission; card payments are Stripe Connect direct charges straight into the provider's own bank — we never touch the funds. (2) **Your brand.** Parents book, pay and get updates under the provider's name, never ours; no marketplace renting them their own customers back. (3) **Your own AI + a full back office.** A role-scoped AI assistant grounded in live data, plus registers, ratios, safeguarding, staff, payroll and a franchise layer that generic booking tools simply don't have.

**Brand voice.** Confident, plain-spoken, on the provider's side — a fellow operator who's done the 6am register, not a SaaS marketer. Active voice, short sentences, UK spelling, "you" and "your". Concrete over clever ("£29 a month, no commission" beats "flexible pricing solutions"). No jargon — no "seamless", "empower", "end-to-end", "solutions". Money and child safety are the emotional core.

**Why we're rebuilding.** The current site reads like every other "booking software" page and buries the only three things that make us different — the money is yours, the brand is yours, and you get an AI team and a full operating system, not a checkout. The rebuild must **lead with positioning, not a feature list.**

### The AI story
Rivals bolt a generic bot onto a knowledge base. Because we own the operational data surface — registers, takings, staff, safeguarding, the whole network — our assistant can stand on live, role-scoped data and answer *your* questions, then link you to the screen to act. It's a category difference, not a feature tick, and it compounds every other edge: rivals have no rich operational data for an AI to stand on in the first place.

### Rationale for the big content choices
- **Lead with money and brand, not features.** They're the emotional core (keep your income, own your customers) and the two clearest wedges against every rival.
- **Reframe as an operating system.** The breadth (day-running + HR + franchise) is what justifies a flat subscription instead of a commission — so Platform overview and the persona pages carry as much weight as Home.
- **Franchise is the moat.** No competitor models the HO→franchisee relationship; give it a full page.
- **Honesty guards baked into copy** so every claim survives a demo: TFC is a *selectable* method today with full reconciliation shipping ~early Sep 2026 (say "accepts TFC and vouchers", not "fully automated TFC reconciliation"); custom domains/subdomains are positioned, not live (one storefront per tenant today); membership recurring Stripe billing is Phase 2 (say "membership plans", not "auto-billed subscriptions"); SMS is a plan allowance, not a live broadcast channel; payroll/leave/timeclock/appraisals/LMS are demo-complete with backend persistence in progress (onboarding, availability and DBS-aware rostering are genuinely wired — lead the proof with what's enforced today); of the named AI roster, only the co-pilot and comms writer are shipped — the rest are "coming soon"; the parent app is a branded responsive web app, not yet a home-screen PWA; automated Stripe card billing for subscriptions is a later milestone.
