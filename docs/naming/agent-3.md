# Naming agent 3: compound and benefit-suggestive names

Screening run overnight 30 Sep to 1 Oct 2026. This is NOT legal clearance. Read-only checks only; nothing was registered, bought or contacted.

## 1. Product understanding

ActivityOS is a multi-tenant UK platform for children's activity and childcare providers: solo freelancers, multi-site companies (holiday camps, after-school and breakfast clubs, schools) and franchise networks. Six portals (company, franchise, freelancer, staff, parent "custdash", platform HQ) sit on one API. Modules: blocks and listings, bookings, waitlists and cancellations, registers and timetables, Stripe Connect direct payments (no commission, provider's own bank), Tax-Free Childcare, trips consent, child profiles, payroll and payslips, Xero/QuickBooks/Sage links, messaging and newsfeed, invoices, meals, medication and safeguarding incidents, leads/CRM, and a Teaching/Learning Hub (lessons, worksheets, quizzes, games for children). The UI ships in 11 languages (en ar ur pl ro cy bn pa pt es fr). The repo's own website plan (docs/website-content-plan-v2.md) calls naming a launch blocker: positioning is "your money, your brand, your back office" so the product name must sit quietly behind the provider's brand, feel warm and trustworthy, and work as a suite (Book / Pay / Learn / Register). The plan also notes a near-collision between "ActivityOS" and a schools-only activityos.co.uk, and "Activly/Actively" appears in docs as an earlier site name. Tone: plain-spoken, UK, on the provider's side, parents and children in the loop.

## Method and honesty notes

- Companies House: fetched the public search page (HTML) for every name. Lists below are the top hits returned; the search is fuzzy, so absence of hits is meaningful but not proof.
- Domains: RDAP status (Verisign for .com, Nominet for .co.uk and .uk, Google registry for .app) plus `dig NS`. 200 = registered. 404 = "appears free" (still not proof; premium or reserved names can also 404). **Every .com was registered** (most coined compounds are). .io and .co were unreliable (rate-limited or rdap.org redirects), so they are reported as unknown.
- UK IPO trade mark search sits behind a captcha for scripted access: NOT READABLE. TMview API also unreachable from this environment. Trade mark evidence is therefore only WebSearch hits (which surface UK IPO journal pages and some US/AU registers patchily). Because the core trade mark sources were unreadable, no name can honestly be rated GREEN; the best are AMBER (leading). Do a professional search in classes 9, 35, 41, 42, 43, 45 before committing.
- Web conflicts: one or two WebSearch queries per name. "No result found" is weak evidence, not proof.
- Language check is my own reading knowledge, not native-speaker review.

## 3. Best 12, full evidence

Scoring: clearance 30, distinctiveness 20, memorability 15, meaning/fit 15, 11-language safety 10, extendability 10. Ranking: Brightbell 74, Hivetime 73, Afterbell 73, Playbell 72, Afterhive 72, Clubcove 72, Bellwise 72, Registerly 71, Coatpeg 67, Fillclub 66, Clubkeep 65, Pegwise 63.

Common to all 12: trade mark register UK IPO not readable (captcha), TMview unreachable; only WebSearch used. Companies House was read directly. .com is registered for every one of them, so the realistic domain plan is .co.uk + .app/.uk (or a prefix such as get or use on the .com, to be decided).

### 3.1 Brightbell (74, AMBER leading)
- Rationale: a bright school bell; warm, child-friendly, evokes "the bell rings, club starts". Two plain words joined, easy to spell and say.
- Sub-brands: Brightbell Book, Pay, Register, Learn, Team. Mascot/logo: a bell.
- Companies House: only BRIGHTBELL LIMITED 01904625, dissolved 23 June 2015 (plus unrelated IS Pharma hit).
- Trade mark: sources read = none of the registers directly (IPO captcha, TMview unreachable); WebSearch found no "Brightbell" mark, but returned BrightBee (US ed-tech staffing app), a different word.
- Domains: .com registered (NS netnerd.com, use unknown); .co.uk appears free; .uk appears free; .app appears free; .io/.co unknown.
- Web conflicts: none found for childcare or booking software. "Brightwheel" (US childcare software) is a near-sounding famous product, though a different word; a risk of confusion in speech.
- Languages: nothing negative seen in any of the 11; "bell" is a universal concept. Arabic/Urdu script transliteration is fine.
- Verdict: strongest all-rounder. Main risk is unread trade mark registers and Brightwheel resemblance.

### 3.2 Hivetime (73, AMBER leading)
- Rationale: hive = busy community; time = term time, session time. Suggests a place where the whole club comes together.
- Sub-brands: Hivetime Book / Pay / Register / Learn; "Hive" is natural for the Learning Hub.
- Companies House: no matches.
- Trade mark: registers unreadable; WebSearch found nothing for the compound but the wider "Hive" space is crowded (Hive by Centrica, Hive PM, Hive AI in classes 9/42), so a class 9/42 search for HIVE-prefixed marks is essential.
- Domains: .com registered (NS ui-dns, use unknown); .co.uk, .uk, .app appear free.
- Web conflicts: none for the exact name; UK children's "The Hive" holiday clubs exist (local, not software).
- Languages: "Hive" ~ "haiv"; Polish/Romanian readers may link to "HIV" sound-alike but weakly; no real negative found.
- Verdict: good, but the HIVE family of marks is the thing to check hardest.
