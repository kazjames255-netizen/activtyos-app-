# Top 50 new-name ideas for ActivityOS (merged from five overnight naming agents)

Prepared 1 Oct 2026. Five agents each proposed 30 names from a different angle (coined words, real words and metaphors, compound names, short global names, trust and heritage names), screened every one, and ranked their best 12 on one shared rubric out of 100 (clearance 30, distinctiveness 20, memorability 15, meaning and fit 15, international safety 10, module extendability 10). This file merges those 60 finalists, drops the 4 rated RED, and lists the top 50 by score.

## Read this first (honest limits)

- **Nothing here is legally cleared.** The UK IPO trade mark register blocked every agent's automated lookup (HTTP 403), and TMview, WIPO and EUIPO could not be read. So every trade mark line is unverified. 'GREEN' in the agents' own labels only means no conflict in the sources they could read.
- **Every short .com is already registered.** Realistic domains are .co.uk, .uk and .app. 'free?' means the registry lookup returned not-found, which is not proof you can buy it (it could be reserved, for sale or parked).
- **Scores are comparable but not identical:** each agent scored its own list, so treat differences of 1 to 3 points as ties.
- **Language checks are the agents' own reading,** not native speakers. Have a native speaker check the final shortlist in Arabic, Urdu, Bengali, Punjabi, Welsh and Polish.
- Next step before spending any money: a **UK and EU trade mark search by an attorney** on the top 3 to 5, in classes 9, 35, 41, 42, 43 and 45.
