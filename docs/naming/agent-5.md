# Naming agent 5 of 5 - Trust, craft and heritage names

Angle: dependable, human, British names (craft, guild, village, lantern, oak, harbour, Welsh/Gaelic/Old English roots, surnames) that suit payroll, safeguarding, accounting and franchise networks and still feel warm to parents.
Date of research: 30 Sep 2026. This is NOT legal clearance.

## 1. Product understanding

ActivityOS (web app; "Activly/Actively" also appears for the marketing site) is a multi-tenant UK platform for children's activity and childcare providers: holiday camps, after-school clubs, schools, freelancers and franchises. Six portals: company/head office, franchise, freelancer, staff, parent dashboard, and HQ/platform. Modules seen in `features/` and `lib/nav/config.ts`: bookings and blocks, listings/storefront, timetables and registers, trips and consent, child profiles, meals/medication/incidents (safeguarding), ratios, payroll and payslips, timeclock, appraisals, accounting links (Xero/QuickBooks/Sage), invoices/payments (Stripe, Tax-Free Childcare), messaging and newsfeed, leads/CRM, referrals, and a Learning/Teaching Hub (lessons, worksheets, quizzes, games). UI is in 11 languages (en ar ur pl ro cy bn pa pt es fr). The name therefore has to carry two registers: serious (money, children's safety, compliance) and warm (parents, kids, freelancers). Key finding up front: every single-word candidate below already has .com and .co.uk registered, so the final brand will almost certainly need a compound, a prefix (get/use/try) or a distinctive second word, or a purchase negotiation.

## 3. Best 12: evidence blocks

Ranking weights: clearance 30, distinctiveness 20, memorability/spelling 15, meaning/fit 15, language safety 10, extendability 10. Clearance scores are capped because the UK IPO register could not be read (see section 4) and every .com/.co.uk is taken.

### 1. Woodwell - 80 - AMBER
- Rationale: wood/oak (craft, growth, playground timber) plus "well" (wellbeing, a village well, everyone draws from it). Friendly to parents, steady for payroll.
- Sub-brands: Woodwell Pay, Woodwell Register, Woodwell Learn, Woodwell Parents, Woodwell Teams.
- Companies House: 16 matches. Woodwell Limited (14415318, active since 2022, sector unknown), Woodwell Property Company, Woodwell Property Solutions, Our Woodwell CIC (2025, community), Wood Wellness CIC, Gemstones Care Ltd (former name Woodwells Care). Sector overlap: none in software/education; community and care names nearby. Exact-name Woodwell Limited needs an SIC check.
- Trade mark: UK IPO register returned HTTP 403 (unreadable). A WebSearch for the name plus classes surfaced only IPO Trade Mark Journal pages with no match shown. Not checked on TMview/WIPO/EUIPO. Nice classes 9, 35, 41, 42, 43, 45 all unchecked.
- Domains: woodwell.com taken, woodwell.co.uk taken, woodwell.app appears free (RDAP 404; one later recheck rate-limited 429), woodwell.uk appears free (Nominet RDAP 404).
- Web conflicts: WebSearch "Woodwell software app platform children childcare schools" found no product of that name; only generic childcare software. Weak evidence.
- Languages (my own reading, not native-reviewed): easy in fr/es/pt/ro; Polish "w" sounds "v" (Vudvel); ar/ur/bn/pa will need transliteration; Welsh "wood/well" fine. No unfortunate meaning known.
- Score: 22 + 14 + 14 + 13 + 8 + 9 = 80. Verdict: AMBER. Best balance; pursue woodwell.app/.uk and do a full UK trade mark search.

## 4. What I could not check

- UK IPO trade mark register: https://trademarks.ipo.gov.uk returned HTTP 403 to my fetcher; nothing was read from it. TMview, WIPO Global Brand Database and EUIPO eSearch were not queried (JavaScript search forms, not readable by fetch). The one WebSearch with trade-mark terms only surfaced Trade Mark Journal pages without a matching hit. So no trade mark statement is a clearance; every verdict is capped at AMBER for that reason.
- Companies House: searched by name only for the best 12 (first 12-15 results read); SIC codes and trading activity of exact-name companies were not opened. The other 18 were not searched there.
- Domains: RDAP 200 means registered, 404 means "appears free" (not proof); some .app checks returned 429 and are marked unknown. dig in this environment returned malformed packets on NS lookups, so DNS is weak evidence. Other TLDs (.io, .co, .org) were not checked. Whether a taken domain is for sale was not checked.
- Web conflicts: one WebSearch per best-12 name at most (six of them searched with product terms; Wrenfield, Bellringer, Tidewell, Clachan, Amberley and all 18 others not). App Store/Google Play were reached only via general web search, not directly. Web search is US-only and returns generic childcare software, so "no result" is weak evidence.
- Languages: my own reading only; no native speakers consulted (bn, pa, ur, ar in particular).
- Section 2 verdicts for names 13-30 rely partly on background knowledge (Fenwick, Marlowe, Cwtch, Rowan, Linden) and are unverified.
