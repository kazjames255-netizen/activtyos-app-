# Naming agent 2: real-word and metaphor names (screened 30 Sep / 1 Oct 2026)

NOT legal clearance. Desk screening only; see "What I could not check". Nothing was registered, bought or contacted.

## 1. Product understanding

ActivityOS (working name; the login/signup pages also say "Activly") is a multi-tenant UK platform for children's activity and childcare providers: holiday camps, after-school clubs, schools, freelancers and franchises. Six portals: company/head office, franchise, freelancer, staff, parent (booking, payments incl. Tax-Free Childcare, trips consent, child profiles) and platform HQ. Modules include bookings and blocks, timetables and registers, payroll and payslips, accounting links (Xero/QuickBooks/Sage), messaging, invoices, leads/CRM, meals, safeguarding incidents and a Teaching/Learning Hub (lessons, worksheets, quizzes, games). The UI is translated into 11 languages (en ar ur pl ro cy bn pa pt es fr), so the name must be easy to say and have no bad meaning in any. Tone wanted: warm, trustworthy, UK, modern, not corporate-cold, not childish-only. Direct competitors seen in searches: Pebble, ClassForKids, MagicBooking, Enrolmy, Xplor, Baluu, Famly, Kinderly. Any name containing "Pebble" is therefore out.

Finding that shapes everything below: for real English words, the .com and usually .co.uk/.app are already registered (RDAP 200). Only invented-compound names (Hazelnest, Meadowly, Kitewing) had any free UK domains. No candidate is GREEN, because the UK trade mark register could not be read (403), so the best possible rating from my evidence is AMBER.

## 3. Best 12: evidence blocks

Score = clearance 30 + distinctiveness 20 + memorability 15 + meaning/fit 15 + language safety 10 + extendability 10.
Domain status comes from registry RDAP (Verisign, Nominet, Google Registry, Identity Digital, .CO Registry) plus `dig NS`. "Registered" may still be parked or for sale, which I did not test.

### 1. Hazelnest: 83/100, AMBER
- Rationale: hazel is the UK hedgerow tree; a nest is the safe home for children. Warm, trustworthy, plainly British, playful but adult-credible. The compound is rare, so distinctive.
- Sub-brands: Hazelnest Book, Hazelnest Pay, Hazelnest Learn, Hazelnest Hub, Hazelnest Families. Mascot fit: a small bird or squirrel.
- Companies House: one exact, HAZELNEST LIMITED (13469274, active, SIC 68100/68209 property). Different sector. Only exact result of the search.
- Trade mark: UK IPO register returned HTTP 403 (not read). A WebSearch for "Hazelnest trade mark" found nothing. TMview, WIPO and EUIPO not readable here. Unverified.
- Domains: hazelnest.com registered; hazelnest.co.uk appears free; .uk appears free; .app appears free; .io appears free; .co registered.
- Web conflicts: no "Hazelnest" product found. Nearby: "Hazel", AI receptionist/CRM for childcare (Capterra), a different name but same field and could cause "is it Hazel?" confusion.
- Language: no known meaning issue in ar ur pl ro cy bn pa pt es fr. "Hazel" is a given name in English. The "zel"/"nest" cluster is easy for Romance speakers; Arabic/Urdu/Punjabi/Bengali readers may add a vowel but it stays readable.
- Verdict: AMBER (trade mark unverified, .com held).

## 4. What I could not check

- UK IPO trade mark register (trademarks.ipo.gov.uk): HTTP 403 from curl and WebFetch. The IPO trade mark journal pages are also 403. I only saw journal entries as search-result snippets, never the marks themselves, so I cannot say whether a mark for any candidate exists.
- TMview, WIPO Global Brand Database and EUIPO eSearch: JavaScript applications, not readable via fetch. Only WebSearch snippets used.
- Whether registered domains are parked, for sale or in use (only registration was tested via RDAP and DNS). The .io registry RDAP answered via Identity Digital; .co via .CO Registry; I treated HTTP 404 as "appears free". Free-looking domains are not a reservation.
- Social handles (Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook, LinkedIn), App Store and Google Play listings: not checked directly; only web search.
- Companies House: searched by name; I read SIC codes only for seven exact active matches. Similar-name lists were shown for the first 20 hits only. Not checked: Scottish/NI registers, charity register, FCA.
- Language checks are my own knowledge, not native-speaker review. Not machine-tested in all 11 languages.
- No professional trade mark search or legal advice. Any shortlist name needs a proper UK/EU search in classes 9, 35, 41, 42, 43, 45 before spending money.
