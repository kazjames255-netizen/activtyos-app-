## 1. Strategy in a page

**What we are.** [Name] is the operating system for UK children's activity providers — one place to sell activities, run the day, pay the team and grow a network, on a single login from a solo coach to a national franchise. It is not a booking widget with extra tabs; it is the back office of a national operator, run from a phone.

**Who it's for.** Three audiences on one platform: the **freelancer** (solo coach, tutor, instructor), the **company** (multi-staff camps, clubs and wraparound care, including owner-operators running several of their own sites), and the **franchise / head office** (a branded network of franchisees).

**The core promise — three things rivals give up on.**
1. **Your money.** No commission. Card payments are Stripe Connect *direct* charges into the provider's own bank — we never hold, pool or pay out your money. You pay one flat subscription and your card processor's standard fee (the same fee a marketplace charges you *on top of* its cut). That last part — a direct charge into your own account — is the piece SaaS rivals who bank your takings first can't match.
2. **Your brand.** Parents book, pay and get updates under the provider's name, never ours. No marketplace renting a provider their own customers back.
3. **A full back office with a live-data AI copilot.** Registers, ratios, safeguarding, staff, a finance hub and a franchise layer — plus an AI copilot that answers from *your* live operational data, scoped by role. A bolt-on help-page chatbot can't do that, because it doesn't sit on the data.

**Brand voice.** Confident, plain-spoken, on the provider's side — a fellow operator who's done the 6am register, not a SaaS marketer. Active voice, short sentences, UK spelling, "you" and "your". Concrete over clever ("£29 a month, no commission" beats "flexible pricing solutions"). No jargon — no "seamless", "empower", "end-to-end", "solutions", and never the word "role-scoped" in customer-facing copy (say what it means: "each person sees only what they're allowed to"). Money and child safety are the emotional core.

**The order we lead in.** Money first, brand second, back-office-with-AI third. The AI is a genuine differentiator, but 2026 buyers are AI-fatigued and a camp owner cares first about keeping their income and filling seats. We lead the demo and the copy on the provable money and brand wedges, and let the AI land as the thing that makes the whole system smarter — not as a six-strong "team" that's mostly roadmap.

**Why we're rebuilding.** The current site reads like every other "booking software" page and buries the three things that make us different — the money is yours, the brand is yours, and you get a full operating system with a live-data AI, not a checkout. The rebuild leads with positioning and *proof*, not a feature list.

### The copilot story
Rivals bolt a generic bot onto a knowledge base. ### Rationale for the big content choices
- **Lead with money and brand, then the back office and copilot.** Money and brand are the emotional core and the clearest wedges; the AI lands as what makes the system smarter, not as a hype-first hero.
