2. Topic Taxonomy — KS2 Maths (Years 3–6)
10 top-level topics, each with a subtopic row per year group (Y3/Y4/Y5/Y6). Objectives below are condensed
from the DfE programme of study — enough for Claude Code (or an LLM generation step) to write calibrated
original questions per topic/year without re-fetching the source document.
Number & Place Value
Year 3
Count in multiples of 4, 8, 50, 100. Recognise place value in 3-digit numbers. Compare/order numbers to 1,000.
Read and write numbers to 1,000 in numerals and words.
Year 4
Count in multiples of 6, 7, 9, 25, 1,000. Place value in 4-digit numbers. Order/compare beyond 1,000. Round to
nearest 10/100/1,000. Roman numerals to 100.
Year 5
Read/write/order/compare numbers to 1,000,000. Count forwards/backwards in powers of 10. Interpret negative
numbers in context. Round to nearest 10/100/1,000/10,000/100,000. Roman numerals to 1,000.
Year 6
Read/write/order/compare numbers up to 10,000,000. Round any whole number to a required degree of
accuracy. Use negative numbers in context; calculate intervals across zero.
Addition & Subtraction

=== PAGE 3 ===
Year 3
Mentally add/subtract a 3-digit number and 1s/10s/100s. Formal written columnar addition/subtraction to 3
digits. Estimate and use inverse operations to check.
Year 4
Formal written methods to 4 digits. Estimate and use inverse operations. Solve two-step addition/subtraction
problems in context.
Year 5
Formal written methods with numbers of more than 4 digits. Mental calculation with increasingly large numbers.
Use rounding to check answers. Multi-step problems in context.
Year 6
Perform mental calculations including mixed operations and large numbers. Solve multi-step problems, deciding
which operations/methods to use and why.
Multiplication & Division
Year 3
Recall multiplication/division facts for 3, 4 and 8 times tables. Multiply two-digit by one-digit numbers. Solve
problems involving scaling and correspondence (n objects to m objects).
Year 4
Recall tables up to 12×12. Multiply two/three-digit numbers by one-digit using formal written layout. Recognise
factor pairs and commutativity. Apply the distributive law.
Year 5
Identify multiples, factors, common factors. Know prime numbers to 19; establish primality to 100. Long
multiplication (up to 4-digit by 2-digit). Short division (up to 4-digit by 1-digit). Square and cube numbers.
Multiply/divide by 10, 100, 1,000.
Year 6
Long multiplication (multi-digit by 2-digit). Long and short division by a 2-digit number, interpreting remainders
appropriately. Identify common factors, common multiples and primes. Apply order of operations (BIDMAS).
Fractions, Decimals & Percentages
Year 3
Count in tenths. Fractions of a discrete set of objects (unit and non-unit, small denominators). Equivalent
fractions with small denominators. Add/subtract fractions with the same denominator.
Year 4
Equivalent fraction families. Count in hundredths. Fractions to calculate quantities. Decimal equivalents of 1/4,
1/2, 3/4. Divide by 10/100. Round decimals to 1 d.p. Compare decimals to 2 d.p.
Year 5
Compare/order fractions with related denominators. Mixed numbers and improper fractions, converting between
the two. Add/subtract fractions with related denominators. Multiply proper fractions and mixed numbers by whole
numbers. Decimals to 3 d.p.; thousandths. Percentage/decimal/fraction equivalence basics (1/2, 1/4, 1/5, 2/5,
4/5).

=== PAGE 4 ===
Year 6
Use common factors to simplify fractions. Add/subtract fractions with different denominators. Multiply proper
fractions. Divide a proper fraction by a whole number. Associate a fraction with division and find decimal
equivalents. Recall fraction/decimal/percentage equivalences.
Ratio & Proportion
Year 3
Not introduced — covered informally within Number and Fractions.
Year 4
Not introduced — covered informally within Number and Fractions.
Year 5
Not introduced as a standalone strand — foundational proportional reasoning built through Fractions and
Measurement.
Year 6
Solve problems involving relative sizes of two quantities. Solve percentage problems (e.g. 15% of 360). Solve
problems involving similar shapes where the scale factor is known. Solve problems involving unequal sharing
and grouping.
Algebra
Year 3
Not introduced.
Year 4
Not introduced.
Year 5
Not introduced as a standalone strand.
Year 6
Use simple formulae. Generate and describe linear number sequences. Express missing-number problems
algebraically. Find pairs of numbers satisfying an equation with 2 unknowns. Enumerate combinations of 2
variables.
Measurement
Year 3
Measure/compare/add/subtract lengths, mass, capacity. Measure perimeter of simple 2-D shapes. Add/subtract
money to give change. Tell/write time from an analogue clock incl. Roman numerals, 12- and 24-hour.
Year 4
Convert between units (km–m, hour–minute). Measure/calculate perimeter and area (by counting squares) of
rectilinear shapes. Estimate/compare/calculate money. Convert time between analogue/digital and 12-/24-hour.
Year 5

=== PAGE 5 ===
Convert between metric units. Approximate metric–imperial equivalences. Perimeter of composite rectilinear
shapes. Area of rectangles (cm², m²). Estimate volume and capacity. Convert units of time. Use all 4 operations
with measures, including decimals.
Year 6
Calculate and convert units of measure using decimal notation to 3 d.p. Convert miles/kilometres. Understand
area/perimeter relationships. Calculate area of parallelograms and triangles. Calculate/compare volume of
cubes and cuboids.
Geometry — Properties of Shapes
Year 3
Draw 2-D shapes, make 3-D shapes. Recognise angles as a property of shape or a description of a turn. Identify
right angles. Identify horizontal/vertical and perpendicular/parallel lines.
Year 4
Compare/classify shapes including quadrilaterals and triangles. Identify acute and obtuse angles;
compare/order angles. Identify lines of symmetry. Complete a symmetric figure.
Year 5
Identify 3-D shapes from 2-D representations. Know angles are measured in degrees; estimate, compare, draw
and measure angles. Identify angles at a point and on a straight line. Use properties of rectangles to find
missing lengths/angles. Distinguish regular/irregular polygons.
Year 6
Draw 2-D shapes using given dimensions/angles. Build 3-D shapes including nets. Classify shapes and find
unknown angles in triangles, quadrilaterals and polygons. Name parts of a circle. Recognise angles at a point,
on a line, and vertically opposite.
Geometry — Position & Direction
Year 3
Introduced informally within shape work — no standalone strand until Year 4.
Year 4
Describe positions on a 2-D grid as coordinates (first quadrant). Describe movements as translations. Plot
points and draw polygons.
Year 5
Identify, describe and represent the position of a shape following a reflection or translation on a 2-D grid (first
quadrant).
Year 6
Describe positions on the full coordinate grid (all 4 quadrants). Draw and translate shapes on the coordinate
plane; reflect in the axes.
Statistics
Year 3

=== PAGE 6 ===
Interpret/present data using bar charts, pictograms and tables. Solve one- and two-step questions using scaled
bar charts/pictograms/tables.
Year 4
Interpret/present discrete and continuous data (bar charts, time graphs). Solve comparison, sum and difference
problems from graphs.
Year 5
Solve comparison/sum/difference problems using line graphs. Complete, read and interpret tables, including
timetables.
Year 6
Interpret/construct pie charts and line graphs. Calculate and interpret the mean as an average.

Diagnostic Assessment — construction rule
Build each subject's diagnostic as a single mixed-topic paper pulling 1–2 questions from every topic across both
the student's nominal year AND the year below. Score is reported per-topic (not just overall), which is what sets
the baseline the mastery dashboard measures future quizzes against. This is a variant of the quiz type — same
schema, type = "diagnostic", topic_id left null or set to a parent subject-level topic since it spans multiple topics.

4. Build Order for Claude Code
1 Topics taxonomy + tenant scoping first — everything else depends on it. Seed the 10 topics x 4 years (40
rows) from Section 2.
2 Course notes + resource library — no dependencies, quick win, validates the topic-filter UI pattern.
3 Quizzes + assessment_attempts + scoring — use Section 3's two topics as the generation template;
extend to all 10 topics x 4 years using the objectives in Section 2.
4 Diagnostic assessment as a variant of Step 3 (same components, different type + cross-topic baseline logic
per Section 3's construction rule).
5 Mastery dashboard — now has real attempt data to aggregate from Steps 3/4.
6 Homework — adds a tutor-marking step on top of Step 3's submission flow.
7 Flashcards + spaced repetition last — self-contained, no other panel depends on it.
Anything not built for the current milestone should render greyed-out as "coming soon" per the existing
feature-toggle pattern — not hidden.
After each step: run node --check if applicable, confirm tenant_id scoping is enforced on every new query,
confirm nav/data-view anchors resolve, and give a bold closing summary stating exactly what was built and
what's next.
