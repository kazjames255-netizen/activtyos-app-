# P-03 Child Home answers "what do I do now?"

Files: `home/KidHome.tsx` (new), `home/StudentHome.tsx`, `progress/KidStars.tsx` (new), `ProgressPanel.tsx` (one early return), `family/KidMode.tsx`, `LearningHubApp.tsx` (tab-strip filter line + import).

- Kid Home is ONE big card (icon, six words or fewer, at least 96px, one 56px "Go" button): "Join your lesson" (only when a lesson's join window is open, checked with the same `lessonTiming` the lobby uses; Join still goes through the existing `/join` token and permission checks), "Your homework is ready", "Play your cards", "All done. Well done!". Two choices made by the builder: a live lesson comes first because it is the only time-critical item; a quiz still waiting shows "Try a quiz" / "Do your starting quiz" so "All done" is never false.
- A failed homework load shows the face and Try again, never "All done" (P-04).
- Hidden in kid mode: streak, level/mastery ring, week dots, stats, flashcard and homework cards. No animation, no countdown.
- Under the card: KS1 (Reception to Year 2) nothing; KS2 (Years 3 to 6) up to 4 short rows (homework, cards, next lesson, last result); Year 7+ a plain "Due this week" list. Band comes from the enrolment `yearGroup`; unreadable = KS2. The remote-sync join banner is unchanged.
- KID_TABS gains only `live` and `dashboard`; `KID_STRIP_HIDDEN` keeps them off the tab strip, so they are reached from the Home card / lesson links only (spec assertions on the visible kid tabs are unchanged).
- Kid Progress = `KidStars`: one row per subject with 1 to 3 stars (70%+ = 3, 40%+ = 2, started = 1), child-scoped read of `/mastery`, no percentages, no chat, no free text.
- Parent Home leads with a verdict line: "On track. Nothing is overdue" or "N homework tasks overdue" (or "We couldn't check homework just now").
- The kid live overlay (z-350) sits above the kid layer (z-320).
