# C1 journal: Year 1-2 child (age 5-7), kid mode on a parent's phone/tablet

Walked through code only (no live run, no child login). Refs are `features/learninghub/` unless stated. Word counts are estimates from the strings in the code.
Kid mode = 7 tabs (`KID_TABS`, family/KidMode.tsx:15): Home, Lessons, Quizzes, Starting quiz, Homework, Flashcards, Messages. The way out is a sum ("What is 17 × 8?", family/ParentGate.tsx:14,20), so a 6-year-old cannot leave. That is good.

## Scenario 1: I open the app. What should I do right now?
**Goal.** Grown-up hands me the phone. I want to be told the one thing to do and do it.
**Start.** A full-screen page (LearningHubApp.tsx:218, `fixed inset-0`). Top bar says "Ava's learning" (KidMode.tsx:60) and a small "Grown-ups" pill with a lock (:64). Below it are 7 pill tabs in a row that scrolls sideways. Each is text only, 13px (HubTabs.tsx:113 hides the icons below 1024px; the button class at :~105 is `text-[13px]`). Then the Home page.
**Path.** I don't read the tabs. I look at the big coloured card: "Good morning, Ava." (StudentHome.tsx:132), then "You have 5 flashcards to review and 1 homework task due soon. A few minutes today keeps it all fresh." (:134). Under it are a level bar, a streak box ("Start a streak today", :141) and a lesson box. Then a Flashcards card with a big number and a button "Review now" (:175). Then a Homework card. Then Latest results. Then "How I'm doing". The one "Keep going" card that says what to do next ("Do the starting quiz", "Continue quiz", "Start quiz", :77-80) is the LAST thing on the page (:252). I have to scroll past 5 boxes to find it.
**Taps.** Best case 1 tap ("Review now" on Flashcards) if I find it. If the plan is "Do the starting quiz", 1 tap on the far-away "Keep going" button, then "Start" (TakeAssessment.tsx:230), then the quiz.
**Words read** before my first button: about 30 to 45 (greeting 3, sentence about 19, streak/level labels about 10, "5 cards ready" and "Review now" about 5). I can't read that many. What I really use is the big number, the coloured cards, and the button shapes.
**Audio.** None on the home page. Nobody says "Hi Ava, tap here". Read-aloud exists only inside one phonics widget ("Hear it", lesson/slides/blocks.tsx:236, using `speechSynthesis`). It is not in home, quizzes, homework or flashcards.
**Where I got lost.** (a) Two big buttons compete: "Review now" at the top and "Keep going" at the bottom. (b) The 7 tabs are all words. "Starting quiz" versus "Quizzes" versus "Lessons" mean nothing to me. (c) The lesson box on the hero ("Next lesson") can send me to "Live lessons" (`go("live")`, :148), which is not in my 7 tabs (KID_TABS has no `live`), so it silently drops me at Home (LearningHubApp.tsx:85). That is a dead tap.
**What scared / annoyed me.** "Grown-ups" with a lock looks like a button I shouldn't tap. If I tap it, I get a maths sum and a keyboard. On a bad load, a red box appears (see the error note below).
**If the server is slow or down.** A red strip: "Couldn't load lessons — The server didn't respond within 15s (http://localhost:4000). Is the API running?." (`PartError`, home/homeKit.tsx:130 plus lib/api.ts:130). "Try again" is 44px (:131) but it is the only usable word. I can't read the rest. It also shows a web address, which is scary. The message is written for a developer.
**Confidence 3/5** (the button is obvious once seen). **Effort 3/5** (scrolling, reading, guessing).
**ONE change.** In kid mode, put a single huge "Keep going: <title>" button with an icon and a spoken "Tap the big button" at the top of Home, and hide the streak, level and stats boxes.

## Scenario 2: I do my homework quiz and get some wrong
**Goal.** Do the quiz my tutor set. Feel OK.
**Path.** Home, Homework tab (or the Homework card, :193, which has a `min-h-[56px]` row). The list row is 68px (StudentHomework.tsx:69), good. The list has a heading and 3 small counters ("To do / Awaiting marking / Marked", :104-106). I tap the row, then the detail page: a big coloured header, a status stepper, "Instructions" (small print), "Lessons to do first", then a button "Take the quiz" (:275, `min-h-[44px]`). It sits far down the page, after instructions I can't read. Then TakeAssessment: an intro screen (title, bullets, a small time note, "Start", :230). Then one question per screen.
**Each question.** The prompt is 19px (QuestionView.tsx:35). Choices are large rows, `min-h-[56px]` (:87), with A/B/C letters and text. Picture options are 2-up tiles with a 44px zoom button (:150), which is good. Type-in answers use a small text box ("Type your answer", :114). A number question shows a number pad on a phone (`inputMode="decimal"`, :121). Written questions want me to write a paragraph ("Write your answer here. Your tutor will read and mark it.", :130). Nothing is read out loud. Nothing tells me if I'm right: it is silent while I pick. The "Next →" and "Skip →" buttons are 44px (TakeAssessment.tsx:297). The jump strip of numbered squares (:304) is the only progress, plus small grey text "Question 3 of 10 · 2 answered" (:274) at 11.5px.
**Hand in.** "Review answers" (:298), then "Ready to hand in?" with "Tap a question to go back to it." (:318-319), then "Hand in" (:337), then a popup "Hand in your answers?" with "You still have N unanswered." (:343-346) and "Yes, hand in". That is 3 taps and 3 screens of reading just to finish.
**Result when I get some wrong.** A big score ring and, if I missed the pass mark, an amber "Nearly there" or "Not there yet" (ResultView.tsx:114). Then a paragraph: "You scored 40%, 25 points short of the 70% pass mark. Look back at the answers below, then have another go." (:126). About 25 words. Below it: "How you did by topic" bars with "Worth another look" (:159), then a long list "Look back at each question" (:172). Each wrong item shows a red-ish chip "Not quite" with "✗" (:194), "0 / 1 marks", "Your answer", "Correct answer", and "Why". The wording is kind (amber, not red, "Nearly there"), and that is good. But it is a 25-word paragraph, a percent sign, "pass mark", "points short", all above my reading level. In the lesson Warm-up, a wrong answer says "Not quite" and shows "The answer is ..." with a shake (WarmupStep.tsx:114-116, :80), and a right answer gets "Yes!/Spot on!/Nice one!" (:17). That is the best moment in the app. The real quiz has none of that per question (marking is server-side, so I only find out at the end).
**How I feel.** Right answers: nothing happens. I don't know I did well until the end. At the end a wrong answer says "Not quite", which is fine, but a big ring that says "40%" with "pass mark" is a school test, not a game. If it is only "written" it says "Handed in. Your tutor is marking it" (:109), and I wait with no score.
**Retake.** "Retake" is a ghost button (:251) or, if blocked, "You can retake this in 2h" (:250) or "One attempt only. Ask your tutor if you need another go." (:248). Both are text walls. A 6-year-old would think they broke it.
**If it fails.** "Couldn't hand in" / "Couldn't start" / `The server didn't respond within 15s (http://localhost:4000)...` in a small notice (TakeAssessment.tsx:89,132). My answers are drafted (draft.ts / useDraftSync), which is good, but I can't tell that from the message.
**Words read** before I can answer the first question: intro (title + about 20 words of bullets) + "Start" about 25. Per question: prompt about 8 to 15 words + 3 to 4 options.
**Confidence 3/5. Effort 4/5.**
**ONE change.** Show a tick or a "not quite, try again" with a big face right after each answer (like the Warm-up already does), and turn the end screen into stars plus one spoken line, with "Try again" as one big button.

## Scenario 5: I finished early. What next without getting lost?
**Goal.** I am done and there's time. Something fun, then stop.
**Path.** After a quiz the result screen offers "Back to quizzes" (TakeAssessment.tsx:246) and "Retake" (:251), plus "Review these N topics" (ResultBanner). Neither says "do X next". After a homework hand-in I get a status stepper and go back with the top-left "Back" (StudentHomework.tsx:185). After a lesson, DoneStep shows stars, score, "N XP earned" (DoneStep.tsx:40,49), the mistakes list and a note on flashcards. On Home when everything is done: "Every quiz done — brilliant." "Flashcards are the best way to lock it in. New quizzes will appear here." (:255 area). That is a real answer, but it's 15 words and appears only at the bottom of Home, and only when ALL quizzes are done.
**Where I got lost.** I have 7 text tabs and no "what next" prompt. "Flashcards" is the obvious next thing but I have to go back to Home, scroll to the Flashcards card, and tap "Review now". Or find the tab. From a homework page, the "flashcards" link works by clicking a hidden button through the DOM (`goToTab`, teachKit.tsx:310), and if that fails it says "Open the Flashcards tab at the top of this page." (StudentHomework.tsx:181), which I can't read.
**Flashcards.** Once I start: the card is big (22 to 28px front text), tap to flip, then rate "Again / Hard / Good / Easy" (ReviewSession.tsx, StudentFlashcards.tsx:139). Those four words are the hard part. I can't read "Again/Hard/Good/Easy" or judge my own memory, and the hint "Space flips the card · 1 Again..." is hidden on phones (`hidden sm:block`). When done: "You're all caught up" with a tick and "Nothing due today, Ava. N cards are scheduled..." (StudentFlashcards.tsx:87-88). That is the same wall of grown-up text.
**Taps.** About 3 to 5 taps (back, back to Home, scroll, "Review now"), more if I get it wrong.
**Words read** about 25 to 40.
**Confidence 2/5. Effort 4/5.**
**ONE change.** End every quiz, homework and lesson with a big "What next?" card with two icon buttons ("Flashcards", "Home"), driven by the same `step` logic that already exists on Home.

## Top 10 frictions
1. No read-aloud anywhere except one phonics block (blocks.tsx:28-39). Every screen assumes reading.
2. Home in kid mode is the grown-up dashboard: about 6 boxes and 30 to 45 words before the first button. The one real "do this next" is the last block (StudentHome.tsx:252).
3. Tabs are text-only pills at 13px (HubTabs.tsx:113 hides icons below 1024px). "Starting quiz", "Quizzes", "Lessons" and "Messages" don't tell a child anything.
4. No feedback while answering a real quiz. Correct/incorrect and praise only arrive at the end. (The lesson Warm-up has it: WarmupStep.tsx:17,114.)
5. The result screen speaks in percentages and pass marks: "25 points short of the 70% pass mark" (ResultView.tsx:126).
6. Error copy is for developers: "The server didn't respond within 15s (http://localhost:4000)" (lib/api.ts:130) inside a red box (homeKit.tsx:130).
7. Hand-in is 3 steps of reading (Review answers, Ready to hand in?, "You still have N unanswered", TakeAssessment.tsx:298,318,343-346).
8. Dead-end tap: Home's next-lesson button goes to `live`, which isn't in kid mode, and silently returns Home (StudentHome.tsx:148, LearningHubApp.tsx:85).
9. Flashcard self-rating uses "Again/Hard/Good/Easy" and keyboard hints (StudentFlashcards.tsx:139). A 6-year-old can't grade themselves.
10. Small print for real work: Homework "Instructions" 13.5px and "Take the quiz" is hidden below them (StudentHomework.tsx:221,275). Many controls are exactly 44px with 11 to 13px labels (e.g. question count 11.5px, TakeAssessment.tsx:274).

## Top 5 keep moments
1. ParentGate: a sum, "Wrong answer = a fresh sum", and a Back-button guard so I can't fall out of kid mode (ParentGate.tsx:9-25, KidMode.tsx:33-49).
2. Warm-up feedback: "Yes! / Spot on! / Nice one!", shake on a miss, and the correct answer shown kindly (WarmupStep.tsx:17,80-81,114-116).
3. Kind wording for a miss: amber "Nearly there", "Worth another look", level names "Getting started / Getting there / Got it!" (ResultView.tsx:114, KidMode.tsx:17).
4. Big tap targets where it counts: answer rows 56px (QuestionView.tsx:87), BigButton 48px (homeKit.tsx:119), homework rows 56 to 68px, and kid bar at 56px (KidMode.tsx:57).
5. Quiz drafts, the "Leave this quiz?" prompt, and "Resume" so I don't lose answers (TakeAssessment.tsx:168,353), plus "Continue quiz" surfaced on Home (StudentHome.tsx:77).
