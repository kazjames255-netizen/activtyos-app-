# Appendix — What the critics flagged & how we fixed it

**Average critic score before revision: 5.4 / 10** — Skeptical buyer 4 · Positioning strategist 6 · CRO 4 · Accuracy fact-checker 7 · Brand voice/copy 6 · Completeness 6 · Franchisor 5.

**Confirmed final page list (v2):** Home · Platform overview · AI ("Your live-data copilot") · For Freelancers · For Companies · For Franchises/Head Office · Parents · Pricing · Why us · **Security & data protection (new)** · **About / Why we built this (new)** · **FAQ (new)** · Book a demo/Contact · **Legal & footer (new)**. Phase-two: feature deep-dives, Integrations, Resources/Guides, "alternative to [competitor]" pages. Plus two build-time appendices (SEO/metadata, demo-readiness checklist).

## Skeptical buyer (4/10)
- **Zero proof → proof designed in.** Home proof band (3 named testimonials + a quantified "keeps £X" story + real screenshots) and testimonial slots on every persona page; proof made a launch blocker; founder-operator About page as fallback credibility if no logos yet.
- **No migration story → "Switching is on us" band** on Home + migration sections on Freelancer/Company/Franchise + an FAQ entry, naming Class4Kids/Pembee/Bookwhen/Eventbrite/spreadsheets — "run in parallel, live in days".
- **Half-built features sold as live → "Live today" vs "Rolling out" split** on the actual bullets (payroll/appraisals/LMS badged beta; only DBS-lock, medication lock, cleared-to-start and clock-in marked enforced).
- **Strawman table → specific, defensible rows** ("Register — but no ratio maths / medication lock / DBS-aware rota").
- **Inspection over-promise → "built to help you evidence at inspection"**; "enforced" reserved for server-blocked actions.
- **Demo-only funnel → dual CTA** with self-serve "Start free" primary for freelancers.
- **Vague "From £49" / "keep 100%" → concrete bands + card-fee line** stated up front.

## Positioning & differentiation strategist (6/10)
- **"Your own AI team" over-index → AI demoted to third beat**; hero singularised to "your whole operation in one place".
- **"Royalties collected automatically" → "calculated to the penny"** everywhere.
- **0% wedge (matched by SaaS rivals) → repositioned around direct-to-bank Stripe Connect settlement** as the structurally unmatchable claim; comparison table split into Marketplace vs Other booking software.
- **Five inconsistent AI names → one "copilot"** site-wide; scopes corrected to five.
- **Generic "operating system" heroes → concrete wiring** ("clock-in already knows the rota…").
- **Absolute "the one thing no rival has" → mechanism claim** ("grounded in your live data — a bolt-on bot can't"). Added About/founder page.

## Conversion / CRO (4/10)
- **"Confirm with founder" placeholder killed**; concrete pricing + "No setup fee / cancel anytime / rolling monthly".
- **Self-serve path added**; demo form cut to 4 fields with "what happens next", GDPR microcopy, and a no-signup sandbox fallback.
- **Data-safety alarm answered** with a Security page + trust blocks on the AI and Company pages.
- **Lock-in, switching, parent-adoption objections** each handled on-page.
- **"Proof points" relabelled** and placed below real testimonials; mid-page CTAs added.

## Accuracy fact-checker (7/10)
- **Waitlist auto-fill → one-tap manual offer with expiring hold** (auto-fill → roadmap).
- **"Monthly memberships / recurring revenue" → benefit-on-join at checkout**; recurring billing badged rolling out.
- **TFC "automatic" → "accepted and reconciled" (manual)**; checklist item added to gate the internal dev banner.
- **"Six brains" → five scopes**; **"in your own voice" → "on-brand"**; **"39 courses" → "60+ / full CPD library"**; voice-demo browser caveat + GROQ/Stripe-key + date-stamped-seed items added to the demo runbook.

## Brand voice & copy editor (6/10)
- **Broken sentences fixed** (Pricing subhead rewritten; Franchise eyebrow → "One platform, from solo coach to national network").
- **"role-scoped" jargon removed** from customer copy (→ "each person sees only what they're allowed to").
- **"actually" crutch, "growth on autopilot", "photo moments" cut**; refrain standardised — "A booking widget takes a payment. We run your whole business."; canonical demo question unified ("Who still owes for this week?").
- Added SEO/meta appendix, demo-form microcopy, footer/legal, CTA vocabulary.

## Completeness critic (6/10)
- Added **Security page, About page, FAQ, Integrations section, legal/footer pages, self-serve path, SEO appendix, support/SLA lines, reliability/offline honesty, "nothing to download" line, accessibility statement, "what happens next"** — and standardised the Stripe card-fee caveat on every "no commission" claim.

## Franchisor / multi-site operator (5/10)
- **"Royalties collected → calculated"** with the invoice reality stated.
- **Absolute data-wall "never" softened**; medication oversight badged rolling out.
- Added **migration/implementation story, network proof slot, an owned-multi-site block on the Company page ("Run twelve sites like one"), HO-internal regional roles, set-once-roll-out standardisation, network trust/DPA strip, franchisee-side value section, and a worked network pricing example.**

**Guards preserved:** the full honesty-guards block and the vs-booking-platform table + copilot story are retained and expanded (§4) — now covering waitlists, memberships, royalty calculation and the AI roster explicitly.
