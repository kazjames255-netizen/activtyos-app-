# Critic and fixer pass: Word Class Sprint (Y3-6 grammar campaign)

Artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/E3qHddMA2w7vDqAkTL51eV (same URL, republished; nothing committed). Source: scratchpad `wcs/` (build.sh). Two critics, one pass each: a bored 10-year-old and a UK primary English teacher (KS2 terminology). Playwright, real mouse drag, touch (CDP touch events plus taps) and keyboard-only, at 390x844 and 1280x800, all 25 stages plus Orders and Visits, Explorer and Junior, Calm on and off, sound on. Data audit script run over every item pool (642 items).

## Top 10 flaws found (worst first) and what was done

1. **Soft-lock crash on the Coast (kid and teacher).** Any wrong preposition that had no special flight path on that object threw `TypeError: reading 'x'` inside the drop handler. The round froze and the correct word then did nothing. The data audit found 83 option/object combinations in the route pools (e.g. "under" at the lighthouse, "on" at the bridge), so a kid who tried a silly answer hit it constantly. Reproduced on the old build, fixed with a generic pose fallback for every preposition, re-run: wrong word flies out and back, right word then fills the gap.
2. **Bosses only talked.** Each boss now meddles: Tob swaps the stalls twice and swipes a sorted crate; Captain Contrary spins the compass (a wrong word sends Gus back to the perch and the route restarts, max twice); Madam Muddle tugs the last coupling loose on a wrong pick (max twice); Trader Tob snatches a word off the coin after both sides are placed; Lord Run-On grows a new clause on the end of the ribbon and spills ink on the relative clause in the Grand Sentence. Hits are refunded, so accuracy and stars are not punished.
3. **No currency (kid).** Added Sparks: 1 per finished round, 2 per clean round (+2 clean comeback on a retest, +2 clean boss round), stage, boss and order bonuses, never for speed. Sinks: Steady Shield (10, hold 3: catches the first slip in a round so score and stars stay safe, the word still returns for practice), free Lens (3 sparks instead of costing a slip), six hats/extras (20-100) and four Pip colours (60-200). A near-perfect full campaign earns about 760, the shop totals about 700 plus shields, so nobody buys everything early. Shown in header, in-play chip, results and the Pip tab. Sparks are cosmetic/insurance only, no real money, nothing random.
4. **Close-synonym coupling items (teacher).** "and" vs "so" vs "although" were all defensible in the boss trains, and single items only worked if you guessed the author's relation. Each coupling now shows its job ("Searching was the result of losing them"), boss trains 4 and 5 were rewritten (T4 "the rain stops ... we should still bring our coats"; T5 "the shop was cold ... he lit the oven"), and results name the real terms (coordinating / subordinating conjunction). Audit: every bank word maps to at most one gap.
5. **Wrong or ambiguous grammar in items (teacher).** Comma splice taught as correct ("Look at the stars," said Ana, "they are beautiful"): replaced. "tried to book tickets" tagged the infinitive marker as a preposition: replaced. "Turn right ... and you find" tense slip, and "After lunch we rested" missing its fronted-adverbial comma: fixed. Determiners with two right answers (an umbrella / this umbrella, that hour, this egg): distractors changed. Modal items where must/will both parse, and "It must rain soon" (must is deduction, not prediction): rewritten with a stated degree ("I am almost sure"). "It {} snow tonight" had base verb "be": rebuilt as snow / snowed / snows / snowing.
6. **Word-coin tiles rejected valid sentences (teacher).** "We like a bird" (verb) and "Keep to the right answer" (adjective) were misclassified so the coin gave no face; "I feel well enough" was a doubtful adjective. Tile sense sets fixed. Twelve adverb-scene distractors were adjectives that are also flat adverbs (tight, quick, slow, loud, quiet, safe, strong, steady): replaced with adjectives that cannot be adverbs.
7. **Clause sorter and Grand Sentence (teacher).** Sentences with a relative clause inside the main clause were shown as two "main clause" cars ("The girl" / "smiled proudly"): filtered to two-block sentences (28 to 22 items). "The library which chimed at noon" was buildable: `which` now needs a thing.
8. **Feedback text glitches (kid and teacher).** "Jam! it time. does not work", "day,:", split speech printed as `leave,”said Ana,“before` (no spaces), the results list showing whole word bags instead of the words you mixed up, "Worth another go" losing punctuation on punctuation items, a double full stop in the coupling job line, the sundial flag clipped to "oh no! it ha". All fixed. A jammed stall also refused the correct word for 1.8 s: correct words are now always accepted.
9. **Wide screens and small phones (kid).** 720px column with dead space at 1280: Journey, Orders, Notebook, Pip and Adults are now two/three-column, the play stage puts Pip, the story, boss and tools on the left and the board on the right, the world palette fills the page, sizes scale up. Phones: header pills no longer wrap, scene height capped, sentence and bank compacted, the token pile and sentence rail are sticky so a long drag has both ends on screen.
10. **Calm mode dropped the teaching animation (teacher).** The sentence train and read-aloud simply vanished in Calm. They now play as a colour outline sweep with no movement (no translate, no shake, no music). Verified with `reducedMotion: reduce`: Calm switches on by itself, sweeps run, animation-name is none.

## Content review (teacher pass)

Every pool was read against KS2 terminology and audited by script: exactly one right pronoun per pronoun item, one right determiner, one right modal band, one right tense form, at least one right word for every preposition/adverb/relative item, every Grand Sentence has 9-24 valid builds. Also added: tense names (present perfect, present progressive, past progressive) and "Coins That Change Job" (the old title said adjective/adverb/preposition but the stage also covers conjunctions and nouns). Ten new adjective/adverb/preposition sentences (Test Stalls pool 14 to 24). 642 items: Market 220, Coast 81, City 150, Village 103, Capital 88.

Left as is (defensible, noted): `so` and `yet` are taught as coordinating conjunctions (KS2 does); route sentences on the Coast are settled by the gold ring in the picture, not by the words alone, so it trains the meaning of the preposition but not "read the sentence for clues"; `like`/`after` word-coin faces are hand-authored, not generated.

## Re-critique after the fixes

Re-played every stage at 390 and 1280 in Explorer (Calm and full motion) and Junior (390), then ran a random fuzz (random drags, taps, key presses, Lens/Key/Shield/Calm/Sound toggles, about 40 actions per stage, two seeds, both widths, all 25 stages). Result: zero page errors, no soft-lock, every round still completable afterwards. Also re-run after the last edits: keyboard-only over all 22 round kinds and all 5 bosses (Explorer and Junior), touch drag and tap over 9 kinds, Orders and Visits. Audio: AudioContext is only created after the first tap; no music oscillators in Calm; music stops on leaving a stage; peak about 16 short oscillators at a win jingle at master gain 0.5; zero console errors or warnings in any run.

Still open (in order): (1) a stage is still 8 rounds of one interaction, so variety lives at world level; nothing new happens between worlds inside a stage. (2) Feedback panels are 2-4 sentences and there is no read-aloud, which a bored 10-year-old will skip; a spoken option would help Junior. (3) On a 664px-tall phone the sticky word bank covers the last gap of a 3-gap route until you scroll; it works but is not lovely. (4) Sparks balance is a first guess; needs real play data. (5) Haptics, real touch feel and real speakers are not verifiable here.
